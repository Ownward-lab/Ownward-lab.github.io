Privacy & pilot terms

These are early experiments operated under the OWNWARD LAB project name. OWNWARD LAB is not presented as an incorporated company.

The manifest checker processes XML locally in your browser. It does not send the content to us, use third-party scripts or set analytics cookies. Hosting infrastructure may keep standard technical access logs; we do not currently measure visitor conversions.

The contact buttons open your email application; they do not submit a form or register a signup. If you email us, your address and message are processed through Gmail and may be reviewed with AI-assisted tools to answer your request. Send only information you are authorized to share. Do not send customer records, passwords or confidential course files.

Interest requests create no purchase, subscription or obligation. Prices are proposed test prices; scope, final price and applicable tax treatment will be confirmed before any paid work. No card payment is collected here.

We intend to remove unsuccessful pilot enquiry records from our working records after 90 days. Provider backups and mandatory records may have separate retention. For access or deletion requests contact ownward.lab@gmail.com. No marketing mailing list is created by an enquiry.

Project signals summarize public business-project records. They are not exclusive leads, planning advice or guarantees that a contract is available.
